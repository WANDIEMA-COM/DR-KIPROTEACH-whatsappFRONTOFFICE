About Dr. Kiproteach - Rehabcare

Specialized Neurological Care Built Around Your Recovery

At Dr. Kiproteach - Rehabcare, we believe that targeted, evidence-based neurological rehabilitation can transform lives. Operating out of our fully equipped physical clinic, Dr. Kiproteach delivers individualized care tailored to each patient’s unique neurological profile.

With extensive expertise in stroke recovery, traumatic brain injuries, spinal cord rehabilitation, and progressive neurodegenerative conditions (such as Parkinson's and Multiple Sclerosis), our clinic provides a supportive, safe, and highly motivating environment. Every session is designed to maximize neural plasticity, restore functional movement, and empower patients to regain their independence.

Our Specialized Clinic Services

Every service is delivered 1-on-1 inside our physical clinic using modern neurological therapy protocols.

Post-Stroke Rehabilitation

Focused movement retraining, task-oriented exercises, and neuro-plasticity principles designed to restore gait, arm function, and daily independence.

  • Gait & Balance Retraining
  • Upper Limb & Hand Function
  • Muscle Tone Management

Spinal Cord Injury & Trauma

Structured therapeutic programs targeting core strength, trunk stability, transfer safety, and functional autonomy in a fully equipped setting.

  • Transfer Training & Mobility
  • Core & Trunk Rehabilitation
  • Secondary Complication Care

Cognitive-Motor Retraining

Integrated physical and cognitive exercises that rebuild coordination, reaction time, dual-task ability, and spatial awareness.

  • Dual-Task Balance Drills
  • Coordination & Agility Work
  • Spatial Perception Training

Progressive Conditions Therapy

Tailored maintenance & recovery plans for Parkinson’s, Multiple Sclerosis, and Ataxia designed to slow mobility decline and maintain confidence.

  • Amplitude Movement (Parkinson's)
  • Endurance & Fatigue Management
  • Fall Prevention Protocols

Clinic Environment & Safety

Designed for Accessible, Safe & High-Outcome Rehabilitation

Recovering from a neurological condition requires a controlled space specifically optimized for physical safety, accessibility, and high visual clarity. Our clinic eliminates home distractions and provides specialized equipment.

Wide doorways, step-free access, support rails, and high-contrast floor markings designed for wheelchair users and mobility-impaired patients.

Specialized Rehabilitation Gear

Parallel bars, weight-assisted harness setups, fine-motor exercise stations, and dedicated neuro-physio tables.
